# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.

## v1.3.0

### Alam el Halfa campaign
- Stronger AI at every difficulty: more aggressive fire, objective-focused play,
  wider engagement radius.
- Retreat direction is now always decided by the attacker - including when the
  attacker's own unit is forced to retreat.

### Quality of life
- Updating / reinstalling keeps your profile, avatar, friends and settings.
- Fixed missing-glyph squares (full-width colon) on some phones.
- One-tap room-code copy/paste; touch-based capture and retreat selection.

## v1.2.5

- Touch-first combat: tap the map to pick the occupying unit after an assault
  (or decline); tap to choose retreat hexes.
- One-tap room-code copy/paste (host panel, menu buttons, in-game Room button).
- Wider map dragging; tap-cycling through stacked units.
- Update checks now report results clearly (up-to-date / network failure) and
  try four mirror sources; mirror download link and copy-link options added.

## v1.2.2

- Fixed critical device issues: room-creation failure (missing network
  permission) and rendering on some devices.
- Camera starts centered on your units.
- Full-width parenthesis display fix.
- AI difficulty levels; choose which unit captures an objective.

## v1.1.4

- First public release.
- Four campaigns: Alam el Halfa, Dunkirk, Kharkov, Winter of Stalingrad.
- Single player vs AI, hot-seat, and LAN multiplayer.
- Touch controls: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap to select.
